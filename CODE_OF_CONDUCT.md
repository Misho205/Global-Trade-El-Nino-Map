# Community code of conduct

Be respectful, specific and constructive. Welcome different backgrounds and
experience levels. Critique code and ideas without attacking people. Respect
privacy and cultural differences when discussing countries, borders and climate.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, unwanted sexual attention, doxxing,
spam and publication of another person's private information are unacceptable.

Maintainers may remove harmful content, close discussions, restrict access or
exclude participants according to seriousness and repetition. Explain decisions
where doing so does not compromise privacy or safety. Maintainers follow this
policy too.

Report sensitive concerns through a verified private maintainer channel. No
dedicated confidential contact is currently published; establish a channel before
sending personal details. Do not post sensitive evidence publicly.
